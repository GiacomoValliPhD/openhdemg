## Primary citation

If you use *openhdemg* for your reaserch, please cite our original [tutorial article](isek_jek_tutorials.md#jek-tutorial-article). Any citation will help us to continue our work.

Cite us as:

*Valli G, Ritsche P, Casolo A, Negro F, De Vito G.* **Tutorial: analysis of central and peripheral motor unit properties from decomposed High-Density surface EMG signals with openhdemg.** Journal of Electromyography and Kinesiology, 2023, 102850, ISSN 1050-6411, [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jelekin.2023.102850](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jelekin.2023.102850){:target="_blank"}.

## Method related citations

Some of the methods implemented in *openhdemg* are described in dedicated publications by their developers. If you use these methods in your research, please cite the corresponding article. Doing so helps acknowledge the researchers who contributed these tools to the *openhdemg* project and supports the continued development of open, reproducible scientific software.

### - Common synaptic input

If you use *openhdemg* for the estimation of **common synaptic input**, please cite the following [tutorial article](csi_tutorial.md) as:

*Cabral HV, Valli G, Zanotti R, Delis I, Negro F.* **Estimating common synaptic inputs to spinal motor neurons from motor unit spike trains using openhdemg.** arXiv, 2026, 2606.23066 [q-bio.NC], [https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.23066](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.23066){:target="_blank"}.

### - SVR discharge rate smoothing

If you use *openhdemg* for **discharge rate smoothing** using **support vector regression**, please cite the following [original article](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ac4594){:target="_blank"} as:

*Beauchamp JA, Khurram OU, Dewald JPA, Heckman CJ, Pearcey GEP.* **A computational approach for generating continuous estimates of motor unit discharge rates and visualizing population discharge characteristics.** Journal of Neural Engineering, 2022, 19(1), 10.1088/1741-2552/ac4594, [https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ac4594](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ac4594){:target="_blank"}.

## Other citations

Please note that many of the methodological frameworks implemented in *openhdemg* were originally proposed, validated, or refined in dedicated publications by other researchers. In addition to citing *openhdemg*, it is the user’s responsibility to cite all relevant articles associated with the specific analyses performed.

This may include, for example, the original or reference publications related to motor unit tracking, conduction velocity estimation, ΔF analysis, or any other method used within the software. Properly citing these works ensures that the researchers who developed the underlying methods receive appropriate credit.
